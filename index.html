Why Inspirational Consultations?

It is an incredible privilege to be chosen by someone to work alongside them.

After many years of supporting children, families and professionals, I continue to be inspired by those I am privileged to support. Their strength, their experiences and their desire to grow is what draws me to this work. Inspirational Consultations is about coming together – not with myself as the expert but with me supporting you to explore and grow what you know to be right for you. Be it as a parent, a child, an individual or a professional developing your practice and sense of self.

In a world where there a so many supports, interventions and information surrounding us daily, navigating our way forward can at times become overwhelming. For inspiration to occur I feel we need space. A space to connect, to be heard, to explore and engage in a process that allows you to identify what will work for you - towards your own personal goals, intentions and values. Inspirational Consultations sets out to provide a space for you.

Good to know

What if I haven't had counselling or supervision before? +

That is OK! I am here guide you and to work with you. Together we can explore how I might be of help to you. Finding the right practitioner for your needs is important. If I don't feel that I would be the best fit for you, I will guide you towards a practitioner that may be better placed to provide the support you need.

What is Social Work and what is the difference between a Social Worker and other helping professionals? +

Social work is a tertiary-qualified profession grounded in social justice, human rights and the empowerment of individuals and communities. Social work training puts particular emphasis on advocacy, strengths-based practice, and working with the whole picture: your relationships and circumstances, alongside the issue you've come in for. As part of this holistic approach I may (with your consent) seek to consult and collaborate with other relevant services involved in your care, such as health care providers, school staff and/or other specialists (eg. Psychologist, OT, Speech Therapist etc).

There are many similarities between Social Workers and other helping professionals and I understand it can be quite confusing trying to figure out who is best placed to support you. If you are unsure, please reach out and I am happy to answer any questions you may have.

Do you use a play based approach when working with children? +

Yes! My approach is play based and tailored to each child and young person I see. I will seek to engage a modality that suits their therapeutic needs as well as their interests.
