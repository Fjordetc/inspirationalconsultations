Why Inspirational Consultations?

Inspirational Consultations is about coming together. My role is to support you to explore your own path, whether you're a parent, a child, an individual, or a professional navigating your practice.

There's a lot of advice, information and expectation directed at us daily, and it can start to feel overwhelming. Inspiration needs space: to connect, to be heard, and to work out what will actually help. That's what Inspirational Consultations offers.

About Jenny Biancotti

Jenny is a social worker with almost 20 years' experience working with children and families in Perth and London, including several years within the child protection system in both Australia and the UK.

Alongside this, she has worked as a trainer and facilitator across Perth, Singapore and London, and spent time as a school social worker in Perth. She now runs her own practice, offering counselling, supervision and training.

Jenny draws on her training and experience, and keeps learning and building on her practice. If she doesn't think she's the right fit for you, she'll help point you toward someone who is.

My approach

Grounded in compassion and connection

Jenny's practice is client-centred, strengths-based, trauma-informed and neuroaffirming, and takes into account how culture, identity and circumstance shape each person's experience. She draws on a range of clinical approaches, and is trained in the use of art, play, Lego and drumming in therapeutic work.

FAQs

Good to know

What if I haven't had supervision or counselling before?

That's fine. I'll work with you to figure out the type of support you need. If I don't think I'm the right fit, I'll point you toward a practitioner who is.

What's the difference between a social worker and other helping professionals?

Social work is a tertiary-qualified profession grounded in social justice, human rights and the empowerment of individuals and communities. Social work training puts particular emphasis on advocacy, strengths-based practice, and working with the whole picture: your relationships and circumstances, alongside the issue you've come in for.

Do you offer a Medicare rebate?

Not at this time — I've chosen to offer low-fee sessions without a rebate.

Acknowledgement of Country

I acknowledge the Whadjuk Noongar people of the Noongar Nation, the traditional owners of the land I live and work on, and pay my respects to their elders past and present — and to the world's oldest living culture, with its rich history of healing, storytelling and community.

My own father arrived in Perth by boat from Malta, and my mother's family migrated here from Italy. Growing up, I heard how they were treated when they arrived, including my dad being hit by teachers for not speaking English. What I was never taught was the history of this land's traditional owners, or that it already belonged to them long before my family arrived. That's a learning journey I'm still on.
